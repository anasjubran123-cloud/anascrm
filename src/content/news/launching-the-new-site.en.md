We're publishing the first cut of www.convergent.sa.

This site is a deliberate reset. It treats Convergent Technology Company as
a group — a mother brand with four specialist business units (data centers,
IT infrastructure, audiovisual, landscape) — and routes visitors directly
to the unit that owns their need.

The site is bilingual from the first commit, hardened by default, and
designed to be straightforward to maintain. Case studies and partner
logos will appear here only after the relevant client and partner
approvals are on file.

If you have a project in scope, the contact form goes straight to the
team. We respond to every qualified inquiry within one business day.
